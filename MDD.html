      Music, Dance & Drama

      Discover, develop, and showcase your talents through creativity, culture, performance, and artistic excellence.

      Music, Dance & Drama (MDD)

      At Bishop Angelo Tarantino Memorial Secondary School, Music, Dance & Drama is a vibrant platform where learners discover their talents, celebrate culture, and build confidence through performance.

      Music

      Choir training, instrumental skills, songwriting, solo performances, and musical creativity.

      Dance

      Traditional and modern dances that celebrate culture, teamwork, and artistic expression.

      Drama

      Stage plays, skits, storytelling, and creative performances that educate and entertain.

      Performance Opportunities

      • School Cultural Festivals
      • Visitors' Day Presentations
      • District Competitions
      • National MDD Festivals
      • End of Term Celebrations

      Values Promoted Through MDD

      • Confidence – Expressing talents boldly.
      • Teamwork – Working together for success.
      • Creativity – Exploring new ideas.
      • Cultural Pride – Preserving our heritage.

      Talent in Action
