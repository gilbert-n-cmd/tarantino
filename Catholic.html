Faith • Education • Service

Catholic Faith & Values

Nurturing spiritually grounded, morally upright, and academically excellent learners through Christ-centered education.

Our Catholic Community

Bishop Angelo Tarantino Memorial Secondary School is rooted in Catholic traditions and values. We strive to nurture the spiritual, moral, and academic growth of every learner through a Christ-centered environment.

Daily Spiritual Life

Prayer, reflection, worship, and participation in Holy Mass are central to our school culture. Learners are encouraged to grow in faith and develop a personal relationship with God.

Catechism & Religious Education

Through Religious Education and Catechism classes, learners deepen their understanding of Catholic teachings and prepare for important sacraments.

Pastoral Care & Guidance

Our chaplain and teachers provide spiritual support, counseling, and guidance to help learners grow emotionally, morally, and spiritually.

Catholic Events & Celebrations

  • Ash Wednesday & Lenten Reflections
  • Holy Mass on Feast Days
  • Rosary Month & Marian Processions
  • Christmas & Easter Celebrations
  • Uganda Martyrs Day Commemoration

Service & Outreach

Inspired by Catholic Social Teaching, learners participate in community service, environmental stewardship, charity activities, and outreach programs that foster compassion and responsibility.

Inclusive & Welcoming

While proudly Catholic, we warmly welcome learners from all faith backgrounds and promote mutual respect, unity, and peaceful coexistence.

Our Guiding Scripture

“Train up a child in the way he should go; and when he is old, he will not depart from it.”

— Proverbs 22:6
