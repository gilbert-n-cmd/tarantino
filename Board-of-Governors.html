      Background

      The Board of Governors provides strategic leadership and governance, ensuring that the school remains committed to academic excellence, integrity, innovation, and holistic student development.

      Leadership & Responsibility

      Chairperson: Mr. John K. Mayombo
      Secretary: Head Teacher (Ex-Officio)
      Members: Representatives from PTA, Foundation Body, Staff and Local Government.

      Governance Structure

      Specialized committees oversee finance, academics, welfare, infrastructure, discipline, and strategic planning to ensure accountability and effective decision-making.

      Educational Oversight

      The Board continuously monitors academic performance, resource allocation, teacher development, and innovation initiatives to enhance educational outcomes.

      Strategic Development

      Working closely with school leadership, the Board supports infrastructure projects, partnerships, staff recruitment, and long-term sustainability.
