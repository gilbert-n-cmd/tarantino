      Muslim Students' Association

      At Bishop Angelo Tarantino Memorial Secondary School, our Muslim community thrives on faith, unity, discipline, and academic excellence. The Muslim Students' Association (MSA) provides a supportive environment where learners can strengthen their faith while pursuing educational success.

      Unity, Faith & Knowledge

      The MSA encourages learners to practice Islam confidently, develop strong moral character, and promote brotherhood, respect, and responsibility.

      Activities & Programs

      • Daily Salah (Prayer)
      • Friday Jumu'ah Services
      • Qur'an Study & Memorization
      • Islamic Debates & Discussions
      • Ramadhan Programs & Iftar Gatherings
      • Community Outreach Activities

      Our Values

      • Faith: Strengthening our connection with Allah.
      • Respect: Promoting tolerance and good conduct.
      • Excellence: Pursuing success academically and spiritually.
      • Community: Supporting one another through unity.

      Leadership & Guidance

      The association is guided by a teacher patron and student leaders who organize activities, mentor members, and promote positive Islamic values.

      Guiding Verse

      Indeed, the most honorable of you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous of you.

      Qur'an 49:13
