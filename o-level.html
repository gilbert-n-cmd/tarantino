O-Level Education Excellence

At Bishop Angelo Tarantino Memorial Secondary School, we provide a strong academic foundation through a learner-centered O-Level curriculum designed to nurture critical thinking, creativity, discipline, and innovation.

Our students are equipped with the knowledge, skills, and values required for higher education and future careers.
