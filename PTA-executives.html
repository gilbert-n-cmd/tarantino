      PTA Executive Committee

      Strengthening collaboration between parents, teachers and school leadership to create a supportive environment where every learner can succeed.

      Building a Stronger School Together

      The Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) plays a vital role in promoting cooperation between parents, teachers, and school administration to support academic excellence and student welfare.

      What is the PTA Executive Committee?

      The PTA Executive Committee is a democratically elected team that represents parents and staff, ensuring effective communication and collaboration between home and school.

      Why the PTA Matters

      Through leadership, resource mobilization, and community engagement, the PTA helps improve learning facilities, student welfare, discipline, and overall school development.

      Meet Our PTA Executive Team

      • Chairperson: Mr. John Okello
      • Vice Chairperson: Mrs. Grace Ayesiga
      • Secretary: Mr. Peter Musoke
      • Treasurer: Mrs. Sarah Nabirye
      • Members: Parent and staff representatives serving on various committees.

      Key Responsibilities

      • Organizing PTA meetings and parent engagement programs.
      • Supporting infrastructure and development projects.
      • Promoting discipline and student welfare.
      • Mobilizing resources for school improvement.
      • Representing parent interests in school management.

      How You Can Get Involved

      Every parent is an important partner in the education journey. By attending meetings, volunteering, and sharing ideas, you contribute directly to the growth and success of the school community.
