      Our History

      Bishop Angelo Tarantino Memorial Secondary School

      📖 Our Foundation

      Bishop Angelo Tarantino Memorial Secondary School was founded with a mission of providing quality education, moral formation, leadership development, and service to the community. The school was established to empower young people with knowledge, discipline, and Christian values.

      Growing Campus

      Modern classrooms and learning facilities.

      Student Success

      Excellent academic and moral formation.

      Achievements

      Strong performance in academics and co-curricular activities.

      School Journey

      The Beginning

      The school started with a vision of providing affordable and quality education to students from different communities.

      Expansion

      New classrooms, offices, laboratories and learning resources were added to accommodate growing enrolment.

      Academic Excellence

      The school continuously improved its academic performance and discipline standards.

      Today

      The school stands as a center of academic excellence, character formation, leadership and innovation.

      🎯 Our Mission

      To provide quality education that nurtures academic excellence, discipline, leadership and moral values.

      👁 Our Vision

      To be a leading institution producing responsible, skilled and God-fearing citizens.
