A Life of Service

Bishop Angelo Tarantino devoted his life to spreading faith, empowering communities, and strengthening education. His leadership transformed countless lives and left a lasting impact throughout the region.

Through compassion, wisdom, and dedication, he became a symbol of hope and inspiration for many generations.
